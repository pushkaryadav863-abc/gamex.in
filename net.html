2. Every rupee you donate goes directly to education and skill training for girls who cannot afford school fees.
3. We provide free digital literacy, sewing training, and computer courses to women in rural Uttar Pradesh.
4. Our aim is to make women self-dependent so they can start their own small business and support their families.

9. This fund is managed directly by GameX.in team and every month we share updates of how money is used.

11. Thank you for playing on GameX.in and thank you for supporting this noble cause with your kind heart.
12. Together we can create more jobs, more education and more respect for women in India.
